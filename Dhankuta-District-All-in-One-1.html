THE WHOLE DISTRICT

More than Dhankuta Bazaar.

This site is designed around Dhankuta District rather than only Dhankuta Municipality. The district includes Dhankuta, Pakhribas and Mahalaxmi municipalities plus Sangurigadhi, Chaubise, Shahidbhumi and Chhathar Jorpati rural municipalities. Destination information combines government and tourism sources; local access can change, so verify details before travel.

LIVING CULTURE

Many communities, many traditions.

Dhankuta's cultural landscape includes Rai/Aathpahariya, Limbu, Yakkha, Newa/Newar, Magar, Tamang, Sherpa, Brahmin, Chhetri and other communities. District information records Hindu, Kirat, Buddhist, Christian, Muslim and other religious communities.

Aathpahariya heritage

The Aathpahariya community has a strong identity in Dhankuta. Nepal Tourism Board has highlighted Syaule Sipting Community Homestay and the community's hospitality, traditions and cuisine.

HomestayTraditional dressCuisineHeritage

Limbu & Kirat traditions

Rajarani and surrounding eastern-hill areas are associated with Limbu heritage. Kirat traditions include seasonal celebrations and community practices such as Udhauli–Ubhauli and Sakela.

Newa / Newar heritage

Newar communities are part of Dhankuta's urban and cultural history. Recent heritage programming has featured Newa songs, dances, food, crafts and heritage walks.

Magar, Tamang & other communities

The district is multi-ethnic. Communities maintain their own languages, foods, dress, music, rituals, festivals and village traditions.

FESTIVALS & FAIRS

A calendar of traditions.

Kirat traditionsUdhauli, Ubhauli and Sakela are among the celebrations recorded in district cultural information.
Major Nepali festivalsDashain, Tihar, Ram Navami, Mahashivaratri, Holi, Chaite Dashain, Janai Purnima and other festivals are celebrated across communities.
Mangsire / KopcheNepal Tourism Board reported a six-day Mangsire and Kopche Heritage Festival in Dhankuta in November 2025, featuring Aathpahariya and Newa traditions, stories, walks, cuisine and crafts.
Local fairsDistrict sources also record events such as Madhuganga Mela and community-specific fairs and jatras. Dates vary by local calendar and organizers.
